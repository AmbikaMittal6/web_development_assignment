Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemical Formula: A representation of a substance using symbols for its constituent elements.

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen Gas: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Methane: CH4

Glucose: C6H12O6

Physics

Kinetic Energy: The energy that an object possesses due to its motion.

Mass is measured in kilograms, while surface area uses m2.

Volume is typically measured in meters cubed (m3).

Mass-Energy Equivalence: E = mc2

Acceleration due to gravity is approximately 9.8 m/s2.

Speed of light constant: 3 × 108 m/s

Biology

Photosynthesis: The biological process used by plants to convert light energy into chemical energy.

During cellular respiration, cells break down glucose (C6H12O6) to release usable energy for living organisms.

Quick Revision

Key Takeaways: Always use <sub> for chemical subscripts like H2O and <sup> for exponential units like m2 and m3.
