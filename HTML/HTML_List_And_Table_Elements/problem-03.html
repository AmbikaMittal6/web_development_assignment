Before a Practical Class

Students should complete the following steps before attending a practical session:

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  3. Check the assigned computer or laboratory system.
  4. Listen carefully to the faculty instructions.
  5. Perform the practical activity.
  6. Save and submit the completed work.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain regular attendance.
  • Students should reach the classroom on time.
  • Mobile phones should remain on silent during lectures.
  • Keep the classroom and laboratory clean.
  • Respect faculty members and fellow students.
  • Laboratory computers and equipment must be used carefully.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A classroom session where a teacher explains academic concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students work with computers or equipment.
Tutorial
A class mainly focused on solving questions and understanding concepts.
Internal Assessment
An evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments or activities.
Practical
A hands-on activity used to apply theoretical knowledge.
