GLA University TechFest 2026

Welcome to TechFest 2026 at GLA University. This three-day college festival brings students together for technical competitions, cultural activities, sports and creative events.

Fest Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a student-focused annual event designed to provide opportunities for students to demonstrate their technical skills, creativity, teamwork and talent.

Registration Process

  1. Select the event in which you want to participate.
  2. Open the online registration form.
  3. Enter your name, college ID and contact details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Upload any required information.
  6. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  7. Submit the form and save your registration number.

Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue before the event starts.
  • Students must follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Maintain discipline during all competitions.
  • Do not damage college property or equipment.
  • Participants should respect other teams and competitors.
  • Unfair practices may result in disqualification.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event where eligible students can participate without being restricted to a particular team.
Team Event
A competition in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
A competition in which one participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of submitting participant details for an event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for organizing and managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final position.
