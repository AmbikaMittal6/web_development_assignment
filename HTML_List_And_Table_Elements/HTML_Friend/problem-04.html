Important Buying Factors

  • Processor: Check generation and performance.
  • RAM: 8 GB or more is useful for regular multitasking.
  • Storage: SSD provides faster performance.
  • Display: Check resolution and screen size.
  • Battery: Important for students who carry laptops to college.
  • Weight: A lighter laptop is easier to carry.
  • Warranty: Check the warranty before purchasing.

Steps to Select a Laptop

  1. Decide your maximum budget.
  2. Identify whether you need the laptop for study, coding or entertainment.
  3. Compare different processors.
  4. Check RAM and storage capacity.
  5. Compare display and battery specifications.
  6. Check laptop weight and portability.
  7. Compare warranty and after-sales support.
  8. Choose the laptop that fits your requirements.

Technical Terms

Processor
The main component responsible for executing computer instructions.
RAM
Temporary memory used by the computer while programs are running.
SSD
A fast storage device used for storing the operating system and files.
GPU
A processor designed mainly for graphics and visual computing.
FHD
Full High Definition display with a resolution of 1920 × 1080 pixels.
Refresh Rate
The number of times the screen refreshes every second, measured in Hz.
