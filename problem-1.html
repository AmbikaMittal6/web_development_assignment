G.L.A University (Greater Noida)

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

GLA University’s Greater Noida Campus is the university's first industry-integrated off-campus centre, specifically designed to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world corporate demands. Located right in the education hub of Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, this campus operates under the prestige of the main UGC-recognized, NAAC A+-accredited Mathura branch. It offers highly specialized tech and management programs tailored to today's job market.

Admission Process Step-by-StepAdmission to the Noida campus is fully formalized and merit-based:Online Registration: You apply directly through the official GLA Admission Portal and complete your personal profile.Entrance Test: You must take the GLAET (GLA Entrance Test), which is a 2-hour online exam consisting of multiple-choice questions assessing aptitude and core subject skills. Note: Valid JEE Main or CAT/MAT scores can bypass parts of this or add massive value to your selection profile.Personal Interview: Shortlisted applicants move into a face-to-face or virtual personal interview round.Seat Locking: Once selected, you must pay a formal token amount within 7 days to secure your seat.

CAMPUS LIFE


Campus life at GLA University’s Greater Noida campus is well-balanced, focused heavily on academic upskilling while offering a lively, modern social environment.

Because this specific branch is an industry-integrated off-campus extension inside a major corporate zone, the campus environment feels more like a modern tech park or business hub than a massive, isolated rural campus.

Here is what you can expect from daily student life on this campus:

1. Student Diversity and CrowdHub Location: Being in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida means you are surrounded by thousands of students from other neighboring colleges.

Diverse Peer Group: The campus hosts students arriving from various states across India, making the class environment culturally diverse and excellent for building networking connections.

2. Events, Fests, and Clubs: Clubs Network: You can join technical or creative student forums, including Coding Clubs (working in the Code Xperience Center), Music, Dance, Drama, Art, and Fashion design units.

Cultural Celebrations: The university arranges major student gatherings like Garba Nights, Diwali Melas, and Independence Day functions where students run performance stalls.

No Overload: While there are regular hackathons, workshops, and project showcases, student reviews note it is not an "everyday party" campus; the core focus remains squarely on project submissions and job preparation.

3. Sports & Infrastructure: Smart Amenities: Daily life happens around centrally air-conditioned Smart Classrooms, premium Computer Labs, and central libraries.

Athletic Spaces: For relaxation, the campus features specialized sports arenas and active fields for Cricket, Football, Basketball, and Volleyball, as well as indoor setups for Table Tennis and Chess.

Daily Conveniences: The campus houses active multi-cuisine Cafeterias, canteens, an Indian chaat corner, and an on-campus shopping stall framework for daily student requirements.

4 Residential Life (Hostels & Mess): Living Spaces: Separate housing wings exist for male and female students with spacious dual or triple sharing options equipped with stable power backup and high-speed Wi-Fi.
Mess: The dining halls provide nutritious vegetarian breakfast, lunch, and dinner sets.

While students rate the food as "decent and hygienic," many also utilize local transit lines to visit food joints around Knowledge Park.
