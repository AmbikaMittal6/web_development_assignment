GLA University (Greater Noida)

About the University

GLA University is a private university located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was established in 2000 and is known for its focus on technology and innovation.It focuses heavily on practical tech and management programs, emerging fields like artificial intelligence, and early internship opportunities within the Delhi/NCR corporate hub.

Programs Offered

GLA University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in various fields such as engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and more.


Some of the popular programs include:

•B.Tech. CSE
•B.Tech. CSE-AIML
•B.Tech. Electronics & Computer Engineeringr
•AI-Enabled BBA
•AI-Enabled MBA (Hons.)
•MCA (AIML)

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University is vibrant and engaging, with numerous clubs, societies, and events organized throughout the year.

Academics and Industry VibePractical learning: Weekly guest lectures and tech workshops begin right in the first year.
Small batches: Focused class sizes help build direct interaction with mentors and faculty.
Professional networking: Frequent corporate visits and live projects connect students directly to the Delhi/NCR job market.
Culture, Events, and Activities: Student clubs: Active groups support cultural arts, music, dance, drama, and fashion.
Campus festivals: Intra and inter-departmental fests celebrate diverse regional traditions and technical fests.
Sports and recreation: Facilities support physical fitness, indoor gaming, and athletic events.
Infrastructure and Facilities: Smart spaces: Fully wired, Wi-Fi-enabled digital classrooms and updated computer labs.

Admissions

Admissions to GLA University are based on merit and performance in the entrance examinations.


Selection requires meeting eligibility criteria (minimum 50% in qualifying exams), taking the online GLA Entrance Test (GLAET) or submitting national scores like JEE Main/CAT/MAT, and clearing a personal interview.
