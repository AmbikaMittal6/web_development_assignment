Travel Destination Guide: Jaipur, Pink City of India

Top Attractions

Jaipur is home to majestic forts and stunning historic architecture.

Explore the famous Hawa Mahal, known as the Palace of Breeze.

Culture & Heritage

Jaipur boasts a rich heritage of traditional Folk dance, block printing, and royal Rajasthani hospitality.

Local Food

Don't miss out on authentic Rajasthani cuisine! You must try Dal Baati Churma and Ghewar.

Read more on Rajasthan Culinary Delights.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Jaipur is during the Winter Season (October to March) when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing.

Travel Tips

Important Tip: Always carry extra drinking water and buy combined monument ticket passes at Amer Fort to save time and money.
