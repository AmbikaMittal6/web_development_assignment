Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. All students must carry their valid institutional ID card at all times while on campus.
IDs are non-transferable and must be presented upon request by staff.

2. Smoking, alcohol, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited on campus grounds.
Violations will result in an immediate 30-day suspension and loss of campus privileges.

Attendance

3. Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all enrolled courses.
Medical leave certificates must be submitted within 3 working days of absence.

Examination

4. Electronic devices including smartwatches and mobile phones are strictly forbidden in examination halls.
Possession of unauthorized materials during tests is treated as academic dishonesty.

Assignment Submission

5. Assignments must be submitted online through the student portal by 11:59 PM on the specified due date.
Late submissions will incur a penalty of 10% deduction per day past the deadline.

Discipline

6. Academic dishonesty, including plagiarism, will result in immediate failure of the coursework.
Repeated disciplinary offenses will lead to a mandatory expulsion review by the academic board.
