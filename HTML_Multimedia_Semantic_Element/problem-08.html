Personal Finance Structural Vocabulary

Interest Rate
The structural percentage value calculated where annual asset generation × baseline capital value bounds.
EMI
Equated Monthly Installment — A systematic transaction checking loan repayment loops monthly until the balance reaches zero.
Principal
The absolute baseline currency sum borrowed from banking channels before adding ongoing interest percentage logs.
Tenure
The designated temporal calendar structure tracking how long capital loan pipelines remain open for repayment checks.
