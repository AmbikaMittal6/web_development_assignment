Preparation Guide & Core Definitions

Essential Preparation Tips

  • Update your digital resume & LinkedIn profile.
  • Research the participating companies “Core Missions”.
  • Prepare a crisp, 30-second elevator pitch.
  • Dress in professional business-casual attire.

Your Attendee Journey Steps

  1. Register online to get your digital entry barcode.
  2. Check-in at the primary welcome desk to collect your badge.
  3. Attend the key opening industry-leader panels.
  4. Visit the organization booths & drop your resume.
  5. Participate in focused, hands-on workshop breakout tracks.

Core Expo Terminology Map

Career Expo
A centralized event bringing together employers, recruiters, and schools to meet job seekers.
Internship
A temporary professional position emphasizing structured on-the-job training < typically 2–6 months >.
Placement
A structured process mapping graduating students directly to full-time corporate employment roles.
Workshop
An intensive interactive training session focused on practical skills development.
Certification
A verified credential validating specific domain expertise or technical skills mastering.
Networking
The interactive exchange of information to build strategic professional relationships.
